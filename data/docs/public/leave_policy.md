# Acme Technologies: Leave Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: People Team (hr@acme.local)

## Annual leave

- Confirmed employees get **18 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per month.
- Employees on probation earn leave from their first month but may use at most 3 days before confirmation.
- Up to **10 unused days** can be carried forward to the next year. Anything above 10 days lapses on 31 December.
- Leave of more than 5 consecutive working days must be requested at least **2 weeks in advance**.

## Sick leave

- Employees get **8 days of paid sick leave** per year. Sick leave does not carry forward.
- A doctor's note is needed for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Public holidays

Acme observes 10 public holidays a year. See the Holiday Calendar 2026 for dates.

## Parental leave

- Birth mothers: 26 weeks of paid leave.
- Other parents (including adoptive parents): 4 weeks of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## Bereavement leave

Up to 5 days of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member.

## How to apply

1. Apply in the HR portal at least 2 working days before the leave starts (except sick leave).
2. Your manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days.
3. For questions about your balance, email hr@acme.local.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be granted in special cases with approval from your manager and the Head of People.
