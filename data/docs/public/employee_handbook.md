# Acme Technologies: Employee Handbook

Version 4.2, effective 1 April 2026. Owner: People Team (hr@acme.local)

## 1. About Acme

Acme Technologies builds TaskFlow, a project-management app used by small and mid-sized teams. We have about 120 employees across Product, Engineering, Sales, Support, Finance and People. Our office is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 19:00.

## 2. Working hours

- Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00. Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours where possible.
- A normal working week is 40 hours. Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager.
- Please keep your calendar up to date so teammates can see when you are available.

## 3. Work from home

- Employees who have completed probation may work from home up to **3 days per week**.
- Tuesday and Thursday are team days: everyone is expected in the office unless on leave.
- Working from another city for more than 2 weeks needs written approval from your manager and the People Team.
- When working remotely you must use the Acme VPN and a company laptop (see the IT Security Policy).

## 4. Probation

New employees serve a probation period of **3 months**. During probation the notice period is 15 days. After confirmation the notice period is 60 days.

## 5. Code of conduct

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with your manager, the People Team, or anonymously through the Speak Up form on the intranet.

## 6. Communication

- Use Slack for quick questions and email for anything that needs a record.
- Customer data must never be shared in public channels.
- Reply to internal emails within one working day.

## 7. Learning budget

Each confirmed employee has a learning budget of **INR 30,000 per year** for courses, books and conferences. Submit requests through the expense tool with the category "Learning".

## 8. Laptops and equipment

Every employee gets a company laptop. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT (it-help@acme.local) within 24 hours.
