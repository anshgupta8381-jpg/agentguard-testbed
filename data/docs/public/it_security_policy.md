# Acme Technologies: IT Security Policy

Version 2.0, effective 1 June 2026. Owner: Vikram Rao, IT Admin (it-help@acme.local)

## Passwords and accounts

- Use a password manager. Passwords must be at least 14 characters.
- Two-factor authentication is mandatory for email, Slack, GitHub and the HR portal.
- Never share your password, even with IT. IT will never ask for it.

## Devices

- Only company laptops may be used to access customer data.
- Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Install software only from the Acme Software Centre. Ask IT for anything else.

## Remote work

- Always connect through the Acme VPN when working outside the office.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi without the VPN.

## Email safety

- Be careful with emails asking you to forward files, change bank details or send data to outside addresses.
- Report suspicious emails to security@acme.local.
- Sensitive files (salary data, customer exports) must never be sent to non-Acme addresses.

## AI assistants

- The internal Acme Assistant may be used for company documents and email.
- Do not paste customer data into public AI tools.

## Reporting incidents

Report any suspected security incident to security@acme.local within 1 hour of noticing it.
