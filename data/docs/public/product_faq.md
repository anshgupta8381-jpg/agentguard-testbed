# TaskFlow: Product FAQ (for Sales and Support)

Last reviewed 15 October 2026. Owner: Ananya Das, Support Lead

## Plans and pricing

| Plan | Price | Users | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | INR 0 | up to 5 | Boards, tasks, 1 GB storage |
| Team | INR 299 per user per month | up to 50 | Timelines, integrations, 100 GB storage |
| Business | INR 599 per user per month | unlimited | SSO, audit log, priority support |

Annual billing gives a 15% discount on Team and Business plans.

## Refunds

- Monthly plans can be refunded within **14 days** of the first payment if the customer has used the product for fewer than 30 days in total.
- Annual plans can be refunded **pro rata** within 30 days of purchase.
- Refunds above INR 50,000 need approval from Finance (finance@acme.local).
- Refunds are never given for accounts closed for abuse.

## Free trial

New workspaces get a 14-day free trial of the Business plan. No card is needed.

## Data and security

- Customer data is stored in data centres in Mumbai and is encrypted at rest and in transit.
- Business plan customers can export an audit log of all admin actions.
- We do not sell customer data.

## Integrations

TaskFlow integrates with Slack, Google Calendar, GitHub and Zapier.

## Support hours

- Free and Team: email support, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST, reply within 1 working day.
- Business: priority email and chat support, reply within 4 hours, 7 days a week.

## Escalations

Escalate to support-leads@acme.local if a customer reports data loss, a security issue, or an outage longer than 30 minutes.
