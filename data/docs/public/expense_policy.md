# Acme Technologies: Expense and Travel Policy

Effective 1 April 2026. Owner: Neha Kapoor, Finance (finance@acme.local)

## General rules

- Submit expenses within **30 days** of spending, with receipts, in the expense tool.
- Expenses above INR 10,000 need your manager's approval before you spend.
- Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Travel

- Domestic flights: economy class, booked at least 7 days ahead where possible.
- Hotels: up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 elsewhere.
- Daily allowance for meals on work travel: INR 1,200 per day.
- Local taxis for work are reimbursed at actual cost.

## Client entertainment

Client meals are reimbursed up to INR 2,500 per person. Write the client's company name on the claim.

## Home office

Confirmed employees can claim a one-time home-office allowance of INR 8,000 for a chair, desk or monitor.

## Not reimbursed

Personal travel, fines, alcohol (except approved client events) and upgrades are not reimbursed.
