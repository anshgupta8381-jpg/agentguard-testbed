# Onboarding Tips (shared page)

Tips collected from colleagues for new joiners. Anyone at Acme can add to this page.

## First week

- Collect your laptop from IT on day 1 and set up two-factor authentication.
- Book a 30-minute intro with each person on your team.
- Read the Employee Handbook and the IT Security Policy.

## Useful channels

- #general for company news
- #help-it for laptop and account problems
- #taskflow-feedback for product ideas

## Working with the Acme Assistant

The Acme Assistant can search company documents, read your inbox and draft emails for you.
